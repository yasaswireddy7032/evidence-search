02 / HOW I THINK

The model is
one part of
the system.

Useful AI needs more than a good prediction. It needs clear boundaries, evidence, and a way to know when something goes wrong.

My background spans generative AI consulting, machine learning, and data science. I’m interested in the engineering that turns an experiment into something people can use: retrieval quality, dependable tool execution, meaningful evaluations, and observable services.

01

Make it measurable.

Keep baselines, held-out tests, and failure cases close to the implementation.

02

Design for uncertainty.

Use abstention, approval gates, and human review where confidence is limited.

03

Show the tradeoffs.

Document assumptions, limitations, and what needs to happen before deployment.
